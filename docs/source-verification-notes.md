# Representative Source Verification Notes

The Rāga foundation record uses a real, clearly identified scholarly reference rather than an invented citation. The source is **Nazir Ali Jairazbhoy, _The Rāgs of North Indian Music: Their Structure and Evolution_**, published by Wesleyan University Press in 1971. Public metadata is available through [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_R%C4%81gs_of_North_Indian_Music.html?id=xsO5AAAAIAAJ) and a digitized item record is available through [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/dli.ministry.26725).

The current product implementation presents this record as a **reference-item demonstration**, not as complete evidence for every claim in the demonstration entry. Future editorial work should attach citations at claim scope, add reviewer notes, and preserve source-specific disagreement where needed.
